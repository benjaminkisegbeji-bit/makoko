01 — ABOUT THE PROJECT

A closer look at Makoko.

Makoko — On the Lagoon is a digital platform focused on documenting and presenting information about Makoko.

The site brings together photographs, maps, stories, historical context and practical visitor information.

Instead of treating Makoko as a single image, the platform gives different parts of the community their own space.

02 — WHY IT EXISTS
Makoko is more than one photograph.

Makoko is frequently represented through a small number of familiar images: houses on stilts, canoes and the lagoon.

Those images are part of the place, but they do not explain the whole community.

This platform was created to make room for a broader view — its geography, waterways, occupations, schools, markets, families, young people, history and everyday routines.

The goal is to provide context around what people see.

04 — EDITORIAL APPROACH

The platform is designed to distinguish between documented information, community accounts and future material still being collected.

01 Photographs should match their captions.
02 Real interviews should be identified as interviews.
03 Unverified figures or claims should not be presented as fact.
04 Community perspectives should be presented with their context.
05 The website should show Makoko as a place where people live, work and move through everyday life.

The website provides a public digital space where information, stories and future initiatives can be presented in one place.

More details about specific Makoko NextGen projects can be added here as they are developed and documented.

06 — BEHIND THE PLATFORM
Built to document Makoko more carefully.

Makoko — On the Lagoon is being developed as an independent digital platform focused on documentation, storytelling and public access to information about Makoko.

The project combines web development, photography, mapping and editorial storytelling.

The platform is intended to grow over time as more photographs, interviews, local knowledge and documented material are collected.
