Before you come

Makoko is home to real people, families and businesses. Please keep these points in mind when planning your visit.

  • ✓ Ask before taking photographs. Seek permission before photographing residents, homes or children.
  • ✓ Arrange local guidance. Contact the team in advance to discuss appropriate local arrangements.
  • ✓ Come to listen and learn. Respect residents’ privacy and avoid assumptions about the community.
  • ✓ Support local services where appropriate. Discuss options with your local contact when planning your visit.
