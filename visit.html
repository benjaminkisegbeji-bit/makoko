VISIT MAKOKO

COME TO MAKOKO start with respect.

Planning to visit Makoko for photography, research, community work or to learn more about the area? Start by getting in touch.

01 — BEFORE YOU VISIT

Makoko is someone's home.

A visit should begin with awareness of the people and environment you are entering.

Makoko is a living community, not simply a tourist attraction.

If you plan to photograph, interview or document people, ask for permission and respect their boundaries.

For organised visits, research, photography or community projects, use the inquiry form below before making arrangements.

02 — VISITOR INFORMATION

Know before you go.

A few things to consider before planning your visit.

01 — LOCAL CONTEXT

Respect the community.

People live and work throughout Makoko. Be considerate of homes, businesses, schools and private spaces.

02 — PHOTOGRAPHY

Ask before photographing people.

A camera does not automatically give permission. Ask people before taking identifiable photographs of them.

03 — WATERWAYS

Move carefully.

Parts of Makoko are connected by waterways. Follow local guidance when moving through areas that require boats or local assistance.

03 — YOUR VISIT

Photography

Visiting to photograph the community, architecture, waterways or everyday life? Tell us what you intend to document.

Research

For academic or independent research, provide information about your project and what you hope to learn.

Community work

If you represent an organisation or initiative interested in working with the community, contact us before making arrangements.

General visit

Want to understand Makoko and experience the area respectfully? Tell us when you are hoping to visit and what you are interested in.
